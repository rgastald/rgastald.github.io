About

I am a Ph.D. student in applied mathematics at CERMICS, École des Ponts (ENPC), and a member of the MATHERIALS project-team at Inria Paris. My thesis, Efficient computation of stochastic dynamics out of equilibrium, is supervised by Gabriel Stoltz and Urbain Vaes.

My Ph.D. is funded by a collaborative CNRS / Imperial College London project, in collaboration with Grigorios A. Pavliotis.

Research interests

My research is driven by physically motivated questions, at the interface of analysis, probability and numerical methods. I am mainly interested in the efficient computation of transport coefficients and rare event probabilities, in interacting particle systems and their mean-field limits, and in quantitative convergence to equilibrium.
